A CLEAR PATH FROM NEED TO ACTION

Every next step
has someone behind it.

One connected response, with a distinct place for every person involved.

01

Tell us what’s needed.

A resident describes the essentials they need. Relay records the request and asks for a delivery point, with permission.

02

Connect the nearby help.

Eligible food and water requests can be matched with verified stock and an available volunteer. Urgent and medical reports need human review.

03

Keep the promise visible.

A volunteer accepts, picks up and reports delivery. Receipt is confirmed separately, while coordinators follow the response.
